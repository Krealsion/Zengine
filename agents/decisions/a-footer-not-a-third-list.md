# A footer, not a third list

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the law it
supports is in [info-controls](../workshop/info-controls.md).

**Context.** `n` and `d` had no pointer path, and the body's row budget was already shared by
two lists (`5f8fab8`, "Give the Info panel two rows a maker can press"). The heading and the
controls then said things in ink alone that ink alone could not carry: `PROPERTIES` wore the
same accent as the selected object above it, and `[ Create ]` read as pressable by a bracket
convention and nothing else (`886e3aa`, "Give the panel's structural rows something to sit on").

**Decision.** The third run is a footer of two pressable rows, reserved by one subtraction
before either list is offered anything and anchored to the foot. The body publishes exactly
`capacity` rows, spare ones blank. Availability is two reasons, one bit, two owners: a live draft
is the application's refusal, made before the operation; no target is the document's, the press
going through. Unavailable is said in characters. The structural rows sit on a ground, and the
ground is presentation that moved no geometry.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *Controls as a third claimant on `share_body_rows`* — rejected: a fixed demand is not a list.
- *Anchoring the footer under the property list* — rejected: the target would move with every
  object's property count, and a control that moves under the hand aiming at it is worse than an
  empty strip.
- *A second region for the footer* — rejected: measured, a whole paint of a 74-row TUI body is
  4.6 µs, so publishing blank rows costs less than `fit_region` read backwards.
- *A `disabled` flag* — rejected: it collapses a fact the application acts on and a fact the
  document speaks for; and availability predicts no refusal (dependents, a spent mint), because
  that would put the document's policy on the paint path; pinned by case `"HD-8: availability
  is not a prediction of what the document will say"`.
- *A quieter ground for an unavailable control* — rejected: it loses the ground entirely, so
  the ground means actionable rather than "a control is here".
- *A `kSectionGround` constant shared by the heading and the controls* — refused: two decisions
  that land on one value are not one decision (`886e3aa`).
- *Keyboard activation for a control* — none: pointer-only, no focus framework.

**Consequences.** `[ Create ]` and `( Delete )` are the same width. The ground reaches the whole
row in both media, and the grounded strip is exactly the pixel partition `prose_row_of_pixel`
inverts; the text inset margin names no control. Every composition number was unmoved across
seven extents when the ground arrived.
