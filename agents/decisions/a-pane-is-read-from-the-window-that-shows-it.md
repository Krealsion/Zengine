# A pane is read from the window that shows it

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the laws it
supports are in [desk-read](../workshop/desk-read.md).

**Context.** A pane that draws in an area of its own draws there, and the weaver sees what the
window composed. The desk read read instead the second picture the pane sent Workshop, which a pane
drawing only in its area does not send, and which says nothing of what the window shows over it.

**Decision.** Workshop asks the window what a pane's area shows (`graphics::AreaShowAsked`), and
answers a read once the window has: its words and its named parts at the area's pixels, after the
window's composition, each part's point where the window's own judge lands a press, its
availability its record's. Workshop keeps its doors, its rate, each row's read authority, its own
words and parts (the band, Layouts, menus, a pane it draws itself), and the covers by its own
order: in the window's answer, an area the asking arranger owns hides nothing. A stamp names a pane by a fingerprint of the window's answer, the window's incarnation
beside it. One version of each shape stands: `DeskRead` v3, `PaneView` v7, `PanePoint` v3,
`DeskStamps` v3, `PaneStamp` v3, `DeskView` v4; the older ones go.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *The window publishing what each area shows after every showing* -- argued against: a message an
  area a showing, and Workshop holding a copy of every picture again.
- *Each office answering its own reading* -- argued against: the owner's word, not what the weaver
  sees; a pane covered by another reads whole until Workshop cuts it.

**Consequences.** A read is a conversation: it waits for one answer a presented pane, at most a few
at once and for a bounded time, and a window started again refuses the reads waiting on it. At the
end of a delivery that may have moved the desk Workshop asks each area again since the version it
took, which costs a message when nothing moved.

**Laws supported.** [WL-READ-08](../workshop/desk-read.md).
