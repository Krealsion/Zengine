# A quit that cannot be asked is refused

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the law it
supports is in [session](../workshop/session.md).

**Context.** Workshop's quit asks every pane holding a weaver's unsaved work whether it may end. A
participant Loom will not deliver to -- held behind a claim its image could not apply, dead, gone,
no longer taking the question -- runs no handler and can never answer; nor can one that returns
from the question with its answer neither spent nor kept. Measured with the real Editor image held
and the question published: the quit waited, every later gesture was held, and the close box went
on saying `quitting -- waiting for 1 pane(s) to answer`. A publication earns no word from Loom
about a recipient it could not reach; an ask earns `zen.AskEnded`, told to the exact asker.

**Decision.** The quit asks. Workshop reads Loom's participants claim, asks each participant alive
whose accept-set names the question, whom a publication of it would reach, through the ask
door as its role, and keeps each ask in its own book. Loom's ending of any of them refuses the
quit in flight, naming the participant, how far the ask got and why, through the cleanup an
answered refusal uses. A participant keeping its answer still holds the quit.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *Tried: the question published, and the host's tap writing each refused delivery in a book
  Workshop read when woken* -- it worked, and it was a pointer into the host that a loaded Workshop
  cannot be handed; a question returned from unanswered still held the quit.
- *Argued: a deadline, a forced exit, or a reload that replays the question* -- outside the
  decision: Loom has no clock, and nothing is synthesized for a silent participant.

**Consequences.** Nothing beside Workshop watches the quit. The refusal names the role, the weave
and Loom's reason; gestures replay; nothing is saved. Coverage is the participants claim's, so a
participant that shows no pane is asked and counted, and one not running is not asked.
