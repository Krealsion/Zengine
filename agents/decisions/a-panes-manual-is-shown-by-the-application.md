# A pane's manual is shown by the application, asked when shown

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the laws it
supports are in [chrome](../workshop/chrome.md) and [desktop-presenting](../workshop/desktop-presenting.md).

**Context.** A pane's weave carries its manual and answers it on ask, and nothing keeps a copy
(`PaneDocumentRequested`, [the manual](../../manual/docs/manual.md)). A weaver needs to read it from
the pane, with the keys they have now, in a terminal and in a window.

**Decision.** Workshop draws a `?` and a gear at the right of a pane's header while the pane's
holder takes the ask and the application declares the row that shows a manual; it reads both before
each paint, and keeps nothing. A press on the `?` performs that row on the pane. The desktop answers
it with a pane of its own, the Manual, beside Hotkeys: it asks the pane's office for its whole
manual, joins it with the keymap the host already says, and lets the words go when its pane leaves
the desk. A key marker's place comes from the build, so nothing reads the page in C++. The gear
opens Workshop's own menu of its settings, a population of its own.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *Tried: the settings as a group in every pane's menu and the room's* — refused: it moves the
  researched populations of those menus (WL-CTX-05), and the cases that pin them went red.
- *Argued: Workshop draws the manual itself, over the pane* — refused: a document reader grown in
  the host where a weave can hold it, and the menu's own precedent is a presenting participant.
- *Argued: the pane declares that it has a manual* — refused: a new declaration where Loom already
  answers which doors a holder takes.
- *Argued: `?` as the key* — refused: a terminal delivers `?` as no key.
- *Tried: `h` in the default class* — refused: a pane keeps every key it did not declare, so the
  row was never asked over the Builder; `Ctrl+f`, above every mode, reaches past it.

**Consequences.** With no desktop there is no `?`. A reloaded desktop shows no words until asked
again. An office offering several panes answers for one; that is the page's to grow.

**Laws supported.** [WL-CHROME-09](../workshop/chrome.md),
[WL-DESK-17](../workshop/desktop-presenting.md).
