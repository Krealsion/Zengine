# A contribution says what it is for, and whether it is offered

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the law it
supports is in [weave](../maker/weave.md); the operator package's is in
[operators](../operators.md).

**Context.** A maker looking for something to compose had identities and signatures and nothing
saying what a power is for. A catalog identity can be covered: an overlay of `math.max` is another
contributor's code, and its words are that contributor's. The maker package mounts each trigger
body as an operator, and a body is one revision's reaction, unmounted at the next edit.

**Decision.** `op::Description` — `about` and `offered` — is authored where the operator is
and held on the `OperatorDef` the catalog stacks; `zengine.OperatorContribution` v2 carries it
across the provider seam and v1 still mounts, saying nothing. `definitions_of` marks every trigger
body not offered. The words and the mark describe and decide nothing: no schema carries them, and
a power marked not offered is spent by whoever names it.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *Argued: a description table beside the catalog* — it would describe whichever contribution
  was in force when it was written, so an overlay would wear the words of what it covers.
- *Argued: recognising a trigger body by its identity's spelling* — a word read to decide what
  is offered is a classification nobody authored, and a rename would move it.
- *Tried: an overlay with its own sentence* — the words follow the contribution in force and
  come back on unmount, pinned by case `"an overlay brings its own words, and unmounting it brings
  the old ones back"`.

**Consequences.** Discovery reads words off the catalog it already reads. A provider's prose is
bounded by `op::kMaxAboutBytes`. Nothing gates on a word.

**Laws supported.** [MW-WEAVE-11](../maker/weave.md).
