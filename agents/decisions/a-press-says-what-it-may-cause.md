# A press says what it may cause, on the row that declares it

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the law it
supports is in [action-causes](../workshop/action-causes.md).

**Context.** An agent that teaches or acts on a desk asks which control causes a message. A pane's
action row was the one declaration its keymap, legend and dispatch shared, and what a press sends
lived only in the pane's code and its manual's prose; a control of another mode was declared
nowhere, and whether a control applied was a pair of parentheses.

**Decision.** Each row names the shapes a press may send beside the pane protocol (`causes`, a
shape, version and subject), and the mode it enters; the declaration names its mode and what each
other mode may cause, never those rows. Workshop judges the form and holds them where it holds the
rows; it resolves nothing. A part says `available`. The census holds the causes, a press of each
action holds them to what it sends, and the manual check holds each `Answers` line's links to them.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *Tried: a declaration beside the rows* — the research's prototype, a side message; it worked,
  and kept a second declaration to join to the first.
- *Argued: Workshop resolves a cause* — refused: a resolver judges by its asker's reads, and
  Workshop would judge scope at a second site.
- *Argued: the cause read from the manual's prose, or a table of control to message* — refused: a
  fact nobody checks rots.

**Consequences.** `PaneActions`, `AppActions`, the parts, `PaneView` and `KeymapShown` each took a
new version; an earlier declaration is read with no causes.
