# Powers are found through one door, and the Terminal may ask it

**Decision record.** One decision, its alternatives, and why this one. Not a how-to — the law it
supports is in [terminal](../workshop/terminal.md); the door's is in [panes](../panes.md).

**Context.** A maker composing in Flow, a weaver browsing in Powers and an agent at the Terminal
all ask which powers there are, by what they are for and by the shapes they take and give. Flow
listed every identity flat from the Flow host's bulk answer; Powers read a whole projection from
the arrangement door and searched it itself. Two derivations of the same facts drift, and the
Terminal could ask neither: the arrangement door answers offices, and the Terminal holds none.

**Decision.** One read-only office, `zengine.powers`, over the live catalog: `FindPowers` and
`DescribePower`, derived at the ask, kept nowhere, bounded, refused in words past the bounds.
It answers any asker with a sender, so the Terminal's own speech is answered. `FindPowers` is
built at its own schema with every field optional, so a line types only what it means. The host
widens its Terminal with the two asks to that office alone. Flow and Powers ask the same door.

**Alternatives considered.**
- *Argued: two more shapes on the arrangement door* — its one rule, offices only, would have had
  to split or loosen for the Terminal, and its one question is the project, not the catalog.
- *Argued: a `ZEN_SHAPE` query* — every field would be required, so the Terminal's composer
  would ask for all nine; a line that types one field could not compose.
- *Tried: the same question from two askers, an office and one speaking for itself* — both are
  answered the same rows, pinned by case `"the discovery door answers whoever asked -- an office,
  or one speaking for itself -- and a root's send nothing"`; and from the three shipped askers,
  by case `"one question gives the same rows to Flow, Powers and the Terminal"`.

**Consequences.** The arrangement door answers the arrangement alone, and Powers browses through
this door; Flow's rail lists only what the door answers, while its graph still reads port
signatures from the Flow host. A query is scalar, so the Terminal spells every one of them.
Paging continues after an identity, a position no door has to remember.
