# Workshop law — a pane's caret

Register `WL-CARET`: the second sentence a pane once said about its rows -- where its insertion
point was, and what it had selected -- and Workshop's judging of it. A pane draws its caret and
its selection in its own picture now, in the area the window gives it (`rows_drawing` and
`canvas_sketch` draw both as marks in the run they stand in), and Workshop judges neither. Router:
[`../workshop.md`](../workshop.md); what a pane draws is [`panes-and-windows.md`](panes-and-windows.md)
(`WL-PANE`) and [`../panes.md`](../panes.md).
