## Questions for the maker (the checkpoint)

1. **Granularity.** 266 rows keep each falsifiable claim with its own witness. A coarser
   register would fold roughly sixty rows into MEANS bullets of their neighbours; the fold
   loses nothing a test pins, since a folded row keeps its witness in the parent's PROVEN BY.
   Which way?
2. **The split** (11 → 25 files) is forced by the 16 KB cap; only the file names are a choice.
3. **LAW length.** About a third of the proposed sentences run to three lines at 100 columns.
   At Step 2 each is compressed or split; a split raises the count by an estimated 10–30.
4. **Overlap with `agents/panes.md`.** WL-PANE-06 (room grants follow the slot) and the
   `PaneWheel` clause of WL-EDIT-10 are stated on both sides of the pane seam today. Proposed:
   Workshop's register keeps the Workshop half (the resolution and the republish moment) and
   `panes.md`'s register keeps the wire.
5. **The two count drifts and three stale identifiers** above are corrected in the entries, not
   in the code; nothing else in the text was found wrong by the tests.
