# Workshop law — participants

Register `WL-PART`: when a piece of Workshop, a pane, a tool, a service or an example is a
participant of its own, and when it is a library of another. One law per heading; cite by ID.
Router: [`../workshop.md`](../workshop.md). The split rule it tests is the root
[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)'s: write Loom, not host code, and split until a piece is too small to
be useful alone.

## WL-PART-01 — A participant earns its place, by measure, or is a library of another

LAW — A piece is a participant only while one holds: two product partners or more, an exercised replacement, an enforced authority or held resource, or a weaver's presence; else it is folded.

MEANS
- each is measured: a seam named in a comment, a same-image reload and `allow_any` count for nothing;
- a pipe is judged by deletion: what its upstream could say to its downstream's door is folded;
- a piece that earns its place and shows a forced sign is given the whole job, not folded.

DOES NOT MEAN — that a folded piece's uses are lost. The snake's controls and clock are the
world's keys and beat; a pilot, a replay and a pace still reach it through `SnakeTurn`,
`SnakeTick` and its state.

WHY — `agents/decisions/a-participant-earns-its-place.md`
