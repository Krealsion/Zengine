# AGENTS.md — Zengine

The start file: an agent's harness loads it when a session begins. It holds no rule of its own.

1. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before you change anything. It is the guide for
   everyone who works here, a person or an agent, and its first part is what any task needs:
   where things live, how to build and test, what a green means, the traps, and how text is
   written.
2. Then read the front page, `README.md`, of the folder your task touches. A folder brought to the
   documentation's design ends it with "Working on it", on the folder's laws, its suites and the
   host's side of it; any other folder keeps that in an `AGENTS.md` beside its front page.

After a summary or a compression of your session, read both again before you go on.
