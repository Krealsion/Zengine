# The action census: every action a shipped pane declares, in every mode its suite drives, one row
# each, `<office> <mode> <id> <default key> <causes> <enters> <label>`, in the order the pane
# declares them. The key is spelled as a weaver's keymap file spells it, `unbound` for none, and a
# row's key for a POSIX terminal follows its default after a `~`; the
# mode is the name the pane declares its rows in force for (the application's rows are `desk`), `-`
# for a pane that declares none.
# The causes are what a press may send beside the pane protocol, `,`-joined, each
# `<shape>/<version>` with its subject after `@` (the part a press lands on, by its name's opening)
# or `>` (one pane, `<office>/<pane>`); `-` for none. `enters` is the mode a press leaves its pane
# in, `-` for none. This file holds the Builder pane's rows and the desk's; each further office's
# are in tests/actions/<office>.txt, in the same form. The census case in
# tests/test_workshop_panes_builder.cpp runs the driver each pane's suite registers (tests/census.hpp)
# and holds every office's rows to what its panes declare, each mode's `elsewhere` to the union of
# the pane's other modes' causes here, and writes the census as it stands beside the build to diff;
# the press witness presses every row of every office, on a pane of its own in the state its suite
# drives, and holds each press's sends to the row's causes. tests/check_manual.cmake holds every
# manual's commands, their Answers links and key markers to it. A pane is listed once a
# manual or a tutorial names its keys. ASCII only.
